# SOC FIELDWORK — Practice Arena Study Guide

The practice area helps you retrieve concepts, connect signals, reconstruct technical sequences and explain real analyst decisions. All tasks run locally with fictional data.

## How to use the six modes

1. **MCQ challenge:** Try the question before reading any reference. After submitting, pay attention to why the correct answer was chosen—not just its letter. The set includes the earlier 176 curriculum questions and 40 new practical investigation questions.
2. **Match the columns:** Choose a term in the left column, then click its meaning on the right. The right-hand column is shuffled for every board. Finish all five connections and check the board. Correct mappings are displayed after submission. Boards cover networking, security controls, Windows event IDs, Sysmon, email security, Kerberos, cloud, hunting and detection engineering.
3. **Put it in order:** Move steps up/down to reconstruct a protocol, investigation, detection or response workflow. Treat the supplied steps as *typical* patterns; production incidents may require adjusted sequencing due to safety and collection requirements.
4. **Myth or fact?:** Decide whether a common SOC assumption is defensible. The explanation covers the critical exception or missing evidence.
5. **Fill the gap:** Recall key protocols, event IDs and technical terms without being shown the options. Answers accept a small set of case-insensitive variants, not arbitrary free-text answers.
6. **Analyst decisions:** Work through case-specific questions based on the existing synthetic incident library. Open the full case when you want the event timeline, raw logs, follow-up notes or analyst debrief.

## 10-question interview sprint

Start the sprint from the bottom of the Practice Arena. Choose your current tier before starting. Answers are locked per question, and the final card provides a score out of 10 and every correct answer with rationale. Your latest 30 sprint scores are saved with your existing training profile. Repeating a sprint is free and does not give duplicate XP.

## Recommended daily rotation (35–45 minutes)

- **10 minutes:** Finish a foundation or L1 lesson, then explain the visual diagram in your own words.
- **10 minutes:** Complete two matching or sequencing boards; note any terms you confuse.
- **10 minutes:** Solve at least five MCQs or myth/fact items; verbalize why the distractors are weaker.
- **10 minutes:** Make decisions in one incident case; cite at least two independent log sources.
- **Optional 5 minutes:** Take a sprint, record your score and review every missed explanation.

## Interview framing

For an alert, answer these five prompts: What happened? What confirms it? What innocent explanations exist? Which log/asset/account would you investigate next? What action is justified under the escalation policy?

**Remember:** A log event can support a hypothesis without proving malicious intent. You should communicate uncertainty, impact, scope, and response authority clearly in every SOC interview and real investigation.
